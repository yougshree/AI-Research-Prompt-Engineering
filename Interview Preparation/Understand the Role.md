## 1. What is AI Research?

AI Research means studying how AI systems work, testing their capabilities and limitations, and finding ways to make them more useful or reliable.

**Example:**

Suppose I want to know which AI is best for writing product descriptions. I could test:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

Using the same prompts and same tasks, then compare:

- Accuracy
- Quality
- Creativity
- Speed
- Cost
- Following instructions

## 2. What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt engineering means designing and improving instructions given to an AI model so that we get better and more consistent results.

**Example**

Bad prompt:
```
Write an email.
```

Better prompt:
```
You are a professional customer-support agent. Write a polite email to a
customer whose order has been delayed by three days. Keep it under 100
words and offer an apology and a solution.
```

The second prompt gives:

- Role
- Context
- Task
- Constraints
- Expected style

So the output should be more consistent.

## 3. What Does an AI Research & Prompt Engineering Intern Do?

"An AI Research and Prompt Engineering Intern would experiment with different AI models and prompting techniques, compare their performance on specific tasks, evaluate the quality of their outputs, document the experiments, and identify practical AI use cases that could improve business processes."

Typical tasks could include:

- Testing different AI models
- Creating prompts
- Improving prompts
- Comparing ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini
- Testing AI accuracy
- Finding hallucinations
- Evaluating responses
- Creating evaluation criteria
- Documenting experiments
- Finding business use cases
- Presenting findings to non-technical people

## 4. How to Compare AI Models

I would compare models using the same dataset, same prompts, and same evaluation criteria. I would measure things like accuracy, relevance, instruction following, consistency, latency, and cost. Then I would analyze the results instead of choosing a model based only on one impressive response.

## 5. How to Evaluate AI Outputs

This is one of the most important skills for the job. AI output isn't automatically correct just because it sounds convincing — you need to evaluate it.

I would check:

| # | Criterion | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accuracy | Is the information factually correct? |
| 2 | Relevance | Does it actually answer the question? |
| 3 | Completeness | Did it miss important information? |
| 4 | Instruction following | Did it follow the prompt? |
| 5 | Hallucination | Did it make up information? |
| 6 | Consistency | Does it produce similar quality across different inputs? |
| 7 | Tone/style | Is the response appropriate for the situation? |

## 6. How AI Can Improve Business Processes

**Before AI**
- Human spends: 2 hours → task

**With AI**
- AI does first draft: 10 minutes → human reviews → final result

The goal isn't always "replace humans." It can be:

> Human + AI = faster workflow

**Example:**

Customer support employee receives 100 questions. Instead of writing every response manually:

```
AI → drafts response → employee reviews → sends
```

**Benefits:**

- Saves time
- Reduces repetitive work
- Increases productivity
- Provides consistent responses

**But we must also consider:**

- Accuracy
- Privacy
- Security
- Cost
- Human review
